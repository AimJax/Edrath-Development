# EDRATH — CANON REGISTRY

**Document:** Project Canon and Decision Log  
**Version:** 2.6  
**Status:** Approved Foundational Canon

This registry records the approved foundational decisions for Edrath, including the 629-root Edraen inventory, compound rules, Edraen Standard Grammar v3.3, the five continents, the initial country-name plan, broad continental climate and geography, and the foundational premise for humanity.

## 1. WORLD AND LANGUAGE

| Concept | Term | Approved meaning or role |
|---|---|---|
| Planet | Edrath | Proper name of the planet; “The Primordial World.” |
| Native language | Edraen | Approved name of the native language. It is an unanalyzed proper name; no root decomposition is part of canon. |
| World (general concept) | Rathaen | “The Eternal World”; general Edraen term for a world or enduring realm of existence. |
| Country | Draumir | General Edraen term for a country or political territory. |
| Continent | Ulmora | General Edraen term for a continent. |

## 4. APPROVED COMPOUND-WORD RULES

Edraen has two approved patterns for root compounds.

### Pattern A — Descriptive compound

**DESCRIBING ROOT + MAIN CONCEPT ROOT**

The final root names the main concept; the preceding root describes or modifies it.

- **ED + RATH = EDRATH** — “The Primordial World.”
- **KHAR + VAEL = KHARVAEL** — literally “stone-heart.”
- **NHUR + AETH = NHURAETH** — literally “night-sky.”

### Pattern B — Concept plus state or quality

**MAIN CONCEPT ROOT + STATE/QUALITY ROOT**

The first root identifies the concept, and the second gives its defining state or quality. In natural English, the resulting meaning may be expressed with the quality first.

- **RATH + AEN = RATHAEN** — literally “world-eternal,” naturally rendered “The Eternal World.”

Both patterns are approved. Pattern B is a productive rule, not an exception invented only for Rathaen. Compounds must use roots whose meanings and grammatical roles make sense in the chosen pattern.

Roots join directly by default. Do not add linking vowels, endings, or sound changes unless a later language rule explicitly requires them.

## 5. APPROVED PRONUNCIATION AND SPELLING CONVENTIONS

These are the current foundational conventions for newly coined Edraen vocabulary:

- The core vowels are **A, E, I, O, U**.
- The established digraphs **AE** and **AU** are permitted; AE is approximately the vowel in English “day,” and AU is approximately the vowel in English “now.”
- **TH** is pronounced as in English “thin.”
- **KH** is pronounced like the final consonant in Scottish “loch.”
- **NH** in the approved root NHUR is pronounced as N.
- The default stress falls on the penultimate (second-to-last) syllable.
- Approved names retain their recorded pronunciation guides, including established lexical pronunciations that do not follow the default stress convention.
- New words should be easy to pronounce, and spelling should remain consistent once a word is established.

These conventions are intentionally compact. They are sufficient for current vocabulary work; a more technical phonetic transcription is not required for ordinary development.

## 6. APPROVED STANDARD EDRAEN GRAMMAR — VERSION 3.3

Edraen Standard v3.3 is approved as the practical grammar for ordinary statements, questions, commands, connected clauses, activity nominalization, counterfactual past, nested relative/content clauses, direct quotation, modal-complement negation scope, and direct-address/vocative syntax. The complete reference is [Edraen Standard Grammar & Usage](language/Edraen_Standard_Grammar.md), with conversational examples in the [Everyday Phrasebook](language/Edraen_Everyday_Phrasebook.md).

### Core sentence order

**SUBJECT + (NU) + (DA or VA) + (aspect/modal) + VERB + OBJECT + OPTIONAL DETAILS**

- Default order is subject → verb → object.
- No tense marker means present or general action; **DA** marks past; **VA** marks future.
- **NU** normally negates the main verb phrase from the beginning of the clause. Inside a modal complement, **NU** may follow the modal to negate only its complement, as in **AN DA PE MO NU TALR GOVR** — “I might not have been able to go.” **LO** marks ongoing action, **PE** completed action, and **SA** habitual action.
- **ES** is the invariant verb “be.”
- Verbs do not conjugate for person or tense.
- Articles and descriptive modifiers precede nouns. Possessors precede the noun they possess.
- **HA** begins yes/no questions; information questions begin with **KA** (what), **KI** (who), **KO** (where), **KE** (when), **KU** (why), or **KAI** (how).
- **RI** marks passive voice; **IF**, **BEK**, **THEN**, **THA**, and **TA** support conditional, causal, sequential, content, and relative clauses. A counterfactual past uses **IF + subject + DA PE + verb phrase**, followed by **subject + DA PE WO + verb phrase** as the hypothetical result.

### Pronouns

| Edraen | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **AN** | I; me |
| **ET** | you |
| **EN** | we; us |
| **IR** | he; she; it; him; her |
| **UR** | they; them |

### Articles, number, and other grammar words

**LI** = the; **NA** = a/an; **MA** = plural marker; **DI/DU** = this/that; **DE/DO** = these/those; **YE/NO** = yes/no. Numbers and the full grammar-word inventory are documented in the standard grammar. **AL/OTH** express all/every and other/another; **BEH/BET** mean behind/between; **SOM/ANY** mean some/any; **SANS/TIL/SIN** mean without/until/since; **ALTH** means although; **DOT** marks a decimal point; **LET** introduces a permissive clause.

### Approved examples

- **AN BAK.** — I run.
- **AN DA BAK.** — I ran.
- **AN VA BAK.** — I will run.
- **AN NU FIRV.** — I do not know.
- **AN LO FARL EDRAEN.** — I am speaking Edraen.
- **HA ET DERV LI VEM?** — Do you want the food?
- **KO ET GELR?** — Where do you live?
- **AN VA GOVR TO LI DARN.** — I will go to the village.
- **AN ZAVN ET.** — I thank you.
- **PLE GARN AN.** — Please help me.
- **IF AN DA PE FIRV, AN DA PE WO TERV.** — If I had known, I would have come.
- **LI DAN TA LAVN THA ET WO TERV DA THULR.** — The person who said that you would come left.
- **AN DA NULR LI DUV BAVR.** — I stopped eating meat.
- **IR DA LAVN: “AN VA THERV NETHL DAK.”** — He/she/it said, “I will return tomorrow.”
- **AN DA PE MO NU TALR GOVR.** — I might not have been able to go.

All grammar words are kept separate from lexical roots. Their spellings are checked against the full root inventory; exact spelling collisions are prohibited. RAETH is a lexical verb, not a new grammar word.

## 8. WORLD DESIGN DIRECTION

Edrath is inspired by the scale, layered history, and cultural depth of *The Elder Scrolls*, but must develop its own identity rather than copy its geography, peoples, lore, or narrative structures.

The aim is a coherent world in which geography, history, politics, language, religion, and culture affect one another.

## 9. APPROVED CONTINENTAL CLIMATE AND GEOGRAPHY

The following broad environmental profiles are approved canon:

1. **Kaldura:** Highland terrain, major mountain chains, grasslands, and strong seasonal variation.
2. **Veskara:** Predominantly arid deserts and plateaus, with seasonal rivers and oases.
3. **Othmere:** Temperate climate with four distinct seasons, fertile plains, river systems, and old forests.
4. **Sylvaris:** Predominantly humid environments, lush rainforests, lowlands, and rivers.
5. **Umbrava:** Cold rugged terrain, glacial regions, volcanic ranges, and severe coastal weather.

These profiles establish broad identities only. Exact coastlines, continent placement, individual feature locations, detailed biome boundaries, and regional climates remain open. See [Continental Climate and Geography](geography/Continental_Climate_and_Geography.md).

## 10. APPROVED HUMAN FOUNDATION

The following human foundation is approved canon:

- Humans are a foundational people of Edrath and are intended to be the setting's **“best” race**.
- Humanity's overall superiority has been established as a fundamental truth from the Age of the Beginning onward; it is not earned later and does not require humans to be smarter, stronger, or best at every individual ability.
- Most widely known great kings and queens, major noble houses, governments, and law-enforcement institutions are predominantly human-led. The degree and form of human dominance varies by continent.
- Humans have multiple subraces with distinguishing physical features, specialties, and other defining traits.
- **Dravkhar** is the chosen name of the preeminent human subrace. Across Edrath's history, it is the lineage most associated with great and historically consequential people.
- Dravkhar have unusually broad potential across virtually any field humans can pursue in Edrath. This potential varies from ordinary to exceptional, with rare outliers capable of extraordinary but finite achievements. Potential does not guarantee talent, mastery, success, status, or greatness.
- Dravkhar's approved core design includes a recognizable but non-mandatory inherited visual signature; ordinary human baseline biology and aging; ancestry-based membership; variable inheritance of appearance through mixed ancestry; moderate population prevalence concentrated in several unspecified regions; and mild tendencies toward determination and competitiveness that are not universal.
- Dravkhar's formal Edraen etymology and literal compound meaning are deferred until the full Edraen dictionary is developed. The existing root **KHAR** retains its approved meaning, “stone.”
- The nature of the underlying quality that enables Dravkhar potential, its supporting lore, the lineage's origins, and the reasons for its historical prominence remain open. Do not assume the quality is biological, magical, metaphysical, or otherwise before that is decided.
- The human subrace design framework may also be applied to other races where appropriate.

The lore supporting or explaining humanity's overall superiority and Dravkhar's historical prominence is deliberately deferred. See [Humanity](peoples/Humanity.md).
