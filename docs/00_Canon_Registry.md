## 4. APPROVED COMPOUND-WORD RULES

Edraen has two approved patterns for root compounds.

### Pattern A — Descriptive compound

**DESCRIBING ROOT + MAIN CONCEPT ROOT**

The final root names the main concept; the preceding root describes or modifies it.

- **ED + RATH = EDRATH** — “The Primordial World.”
- **KHAR + VAEL = KHARVAEL** — literally “stone-heart.”
- **NHUR + AETH = NHURAETH** — literally “night-sky.”

### Pattern B — Concept plus state or quality

**MAIN CONCEPT ROOT + STATE/QUALITY ROOT**

The first root identifies the concept, and the second gives its defining state or quality. In natural English, the resulting meaning may be expressed with the quality first.

- **RATH + AEN = RATHAEN** — literally “world-eternal,” naturally rendered “The Eternal World.”

Both patterns are approved. Pattern B is a productive rule, not an exception invented only for Rathaen. Compounds must use roots whose meanings and grammatical roles make sense in the chosen pattern.

Roots join directly by default. Do not add linking vowels, endings, or sound changes unless a later language rule explicitly requires them.

## 5. APPROVED PRONUNCIATION AND SPELLING CONVENTIONS

These are the current foundational conventions for newly coined Edraen vocabulary:

- The core vowels are **A, E, I, O, U**.
- The established digraphs **AE** and **AU** are permitted; AE is approximately the vowel in English “day,” and AU is approximately the vowel in English “now.”
- **TH** is pronounced as in English “thin.”
- **KH** is pronounced like the final consonant in Scottish “loch.”
- **NH** in the approved root NHUR is pronounced as N.
- The default stress falls on the penultimate (second-to-last) syllable.
- Approved names retain their recorded pronunciation guides, including established lexical pronunciations that do not follow the default stress convention.
- New words should be easy to pronounce, and spelling should remain consistent once a word is established.

These conventions are intentionally compact. They are sufficient for current vocabulary work; a more technical phonetic transcription is not required for ordinary development.

## 6. APPROVED PLACE NAMES WITHOUT ASSIGNED ETYMOLOGIES

The following are approved terms or proper names. Their recorded roles are canon; no internal decomposition is assigned unless explicitly stated otherwise.

- **Edraen** — the native language's name.
- **Draumir** — country or political territory.
- **Ulmora** — continent.

- **Orvane, Kelvarn, Sadrik, Mavren** — country names assigned to Kaldura.
- **Tazhen, Ilyara, Khorvek, Seredin** — country names assigned to Veskara.
- **Brackenfell, Edevane, Morcant, Dunwery** — country names assigned to Othmere.
- **Avenelle, Theryn, Caliseth, Ysolde** — country names assigned to Sylvaris.
- **Dravosk, Keshvar, Oruvain, Varkesh** — country names assigned to Umbrava.

Country names do not need a literal Edraen meaning. Some may eventually be connected to Edraen or other linguistic histories, but no etymologies are established here and none should be invented merely to justify a name.

## 7. WORLD DESIGN DIRECTION

Edrath is inspired by the scale, layered history, and cultural depth of *The Elder Scrolls*, but must develop its own identity rather than copy its geography, peoples, lore, or narrative structures.

The aim is a coherent world in which geography, history, politics, language, religion, and culture affect one another.

## 8. APPROVED CONTINENTAL CLIMATE AND GEOGRAPHY

The following broad environmental profiles are approved canon:

1. **Kaldura:** Highland terrain, major mountain chains, grasslands, and strong seasonal variation.
2. **Veskara:** Predominantly arid deserts and plateaus, with seasonal rivers and oases.
3. **Othmere:** Temperate climate with four distinct seasons, fertile plains, river systems, and old forests.
4. **Sylvaris:** Predominantly humid environments, lush rainforests, lowlands, and rivers.
5. **Umbrava:** Cold rugged terrain, glacial regions, volcanic ranges, and severe coastal weather.

These profiles establish broad identities only. Exact coastlines, continent placement, individual feature locations, detailed biome boundaries, and regional climates remain open. See [Continental Climate and Geography](geography/Continental_Climate_and_Geography.md).

## 9. FUTURE DEVELOPMENT — NOT YET AUTHORED

The following material remains open for future development:

1. The relative placement, shape, and size of the five continents, plus exact coastlines and detailed maps.
2. Precise locations and extents of geographic features beyond the approved broad profiles.
3. Country borders, size, precise placement, governments, and political histories.
4. Final definitions of races and peoples, their relationships, cultures, religions, and civilizations. Initial brainstorming proposals are recorded in [Race Design Workshop](peoples/Race_Design_Workshop.md); none are canon until explicitly approved.
5. Additional Edraen roots, vocabulary, grammar, and idioms.
6. Historical events, conflicts, cosmological details, and narrative lore.
7. Whether additional countries are needed as the setting develops.

The current country plan contains four named countries per continent, for a total of 20. This is an approved starting scope, not a claim that the political history of Edrath has always contained exactly 20 countries.

Do not invent unwritten details merely to fill a checklist. Develop them when the project reaches that stage and document the decisions in GitHub.

## CANON RULE

The decisions in this registry are approved canon. Changes require an explicit later decision. Future content can expand the world without contradicting the foundations above.
