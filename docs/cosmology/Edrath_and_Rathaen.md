# EDRATH AND RATHAEN — WORLD NAME AND COSMOLOGY

**Document:** Planetary Name and World Terminology  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Approved Foundational Canon

## 1. EDRATH — THE PLANET

**Edrath** is the proper name of the planet.

### Approved construction

**ED + RATH = EDRATH**

- **ED** — Primordial; original; that which existed from the beginning.
- **RATH** — World; the encompassing realm of existence.

### Approved meaning

**The Primordial World** or **The Primordial Realm**.

This is the approved literal interpretation of the name. Religious, historical, and philosophical interpretations may differ among the civilizations of Edrath, but they do not change the core linguistic meaning.

The construction follows Edraen Pattern A:

**DESCRIBING ROOT + MAIN CONCEPT ROOT = COMPOUND WORD**

ED describes RATH, giving “primordial world.”

## 2. RATHAEN — THE WORLD

**Rathaen** is the Edraen general term for “the eternal world,” referring to a world or enduring realm of existence. It is distinct from Edrath, the planet's proper name.

### Approved roots

- **RATH** — World; the encompassing realm of existence.
- **AEN** — Eternal; enduring beyond generations and ages.

### Approved construction

**RATH + AEN = RATHAEN**

### Approved meaning

**The Eternal World.**

The literal order is “world-eternal.” Natural English renders the quality first as “eternal world.”

This construction follows Edraen Pattern B:

**MAIN CONCEPT ROOT + STATE/QUALITY ROOT = COMPOUND WORD**

Pattern B is a general, approved compound pattern in Edraen, not an exception created solely for Rathaen. The word describes an enduring realm; it does not necessarily claim that a world is literally indestructible.

## 3. THE DISTINCTION

- **Edrath** — The proper name of the planet; “The Primordial World.”
- **Rathaen** — The general Edraen term “The Eternal World.”
- **Edraen** — The name of the planet's native language.

These terms have distinct roles and should not be treated as interchangeable.

## 4. CANONICAL ETYMOLOGY POLICY

The root meanings ED, RATH, and AEN and the constructions EDRATH and RATHAEN are approved canon.

The names **Edraen**, **Draumir**, **Ulmora**, **Kaldura**, **Veskara**, **Othmere**, **Sylvaris**, and **Umbrava** are treated as unanalyzed whole terms. No root decomposition or hidden etymology is assigned to them in current canon.

A culture within Edrath may develop its own folk etymology, religious explanation, or historical interpretation in future lore, but such an in-world belief must be distinguished from the language's approved literal construction.

## DESIGN PRINCIPLE

Names and vocabulary should work within Edraen's established rules. Words may acquire deeper cultural, historical, religious, and poetic meanings as the civilizations of Edrath develop, without changing their approved root meanings.
