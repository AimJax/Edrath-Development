# EDRATH AND RATHAEN — WORLD NAME AND COSMOLOGY

**Document:** Planetary Name and World Terminology  
**Version:** 0.2  
**Status:** Working Canon

## 1. EDRATH — THE PLANET

**Edrath** is the proper name of the planet.

The name is established. Its proposed etymology is not yet definitive.

### Proposed construction

**ED + RATH = EDRATH**

- **ED** — Primordial; original; that which existed from the beginning.
- **RATH** — World or encompassing realm of existence.

### Intended literal meaning

**The Primordial World** or **The Primordial Realm**.

This interpretation presents Edrath as the original, encompassing realm in which existence unfolds. Its religious, historical, and philosophical meanings may differ among the world's civilizations.

### Etymology status

The name **Edrath** is approved. The root meanings of ED and RATH and the literal etymology remain provisional until explicitly approved.

Under the initial Edraen compound rule, ED describes RATH, so the proposed construction follows:

**DESCRIBING ROOT + MAIN CONCEPT ROOT = COMPOUND WORD**

## 2. RATHAEN — THE WORLD

**Rathaen** is the current working term for the general concept of “world.” It is distinct from Edrath, the proper name of the planet.

### Proposed roots

- **RATH** — World; the encompassing place of land, sea, sky, and life.
- **AEN** — Eternal; that which endures beyond generations and the passing of ages.

### Proposed construction

**RATH + AEN = RATHAEN**

### Intended meaning

**The Eternal World.**

The intended meaning treats a world as an ancient, enduring realm of existence. It does not necessarily imply that the world is literally indestructible.

### Unresolved linguistic question

Rathaen places RATH before AEN. This differs from Edraen's initial compound pattern, in which the describing root comes first and the main concept root comes last.

A second compound pattern may be possible, but it must be explicitly defined before the construction of Rathaen is considered linguistically settled.

The term remains a working choice; the roots AEN and RATH and the compound structure remain provisional.

## 3. THE DISTINCTION

- **Edrath** — The proper name of the planet.
- **Rathaen** — The working general term for “world.”
- **Edraen** — The name of the native language.

These terms serve different roles and should not be treated as interchangeable.

## 4. DESIGN PRINCIPLE

Names and vocabulary should emerge from Edraen's internal logic rather than being invented independently. Words may acquire deeper cultural, historical, religious, and poetic meanings as the civilizations of Edrath develop.

**An approved name can have an unresolved etymology. Do not confuse the two.**
