# EDRAEN EVERYDAY PHRASEBOOK

**Standard:** Edraen Standard v2.0  
**Status:** Approved usage examples  
**Rule:** Every example below uses approved roots and the documented grammar. Where a phrase is literal rather than idiomatic, the English rendering is kept close to the Edraen structure.

## Greetings and courtesy

| Edraen | English |
|---|---|
| **AN GELT ET.** | I greet you. |
| **AN ZAVN ET.** | I thank you. |
| **AN METHV.** | I apologize. |
| **THULM!** | Farewell! |
| **PLE** | Please. |
| **YE.** | Yes. |
| **NO.** | No. |

## Introductions and identity

| Edraen | English |
|---|---|
| **AN ES [name].** | I am [name]. |
| **KI ES ET?** | Who are you? |
| **AN ES SAVTH.** | I am a student. |
| **AN FARL EDRAEN.** | I speak Edraen. |
| **AN TALR FARL EDRAEN.** | I can speak Edraen. |
| **HA ET FIRV EDRAEN?** | Do you know Edraen? |

A name may be inserted as a proper name. Do not invent an Edraen etymology for a name unless one has been approved.

## Basic needs and requests

| Edraen | English |
|---|---|
| **AN DERV VEM.** | I want food. |
| **AN DOVT VOR.** | I need water. |
| **AN HURV MELK.** | I have money. |
| **PLE GARN AN.** | Please help me. |
| **PLE LEVR LI ZULR TO EN.** | Please bring the book to us. |
| **AN NU FIRV.** | I do not know. |
| **AN NU KRETH.** | I do not understand. |
| **KA ET DERV?** | What do you want? |
| **HA ET DUV VEM?** | Do you eat food? |

## Places and movement

| Edraen | English |
|---|---|
| **KO ET GELR?** | Where do you live? |
| **AN GELR IN LI DARN.** | I live in the village. |
| **AN VA GOVR TO LI DARN.** | I will go to the village. |
| **AN DA SAVM IN LI DOM.** | I stayed in the home. |
| **KE ET VA TERV?** | When will you come? |
| **AN VA RAVN ET.** | I will meet you. |
| **IR TERV FU LI DARN.** | He/she/it comes from the village. |

## Questions for daily use

| Edraen | English |
|---|---|
| **KA ET DERV?** | What do you want? |
| **KI ES LI TULK?** | Who is the leader? |
| **KO ET GELR?** | Where do you live? |
| **KE ET VA TERV?** | When will you come? |
| **KU ET NU SAVN?** | Why do you not remember? |
| **KAI AN KERV LI BRUK?** | How do I repair the door? |
| **HA ET ES SETH?** | Are you well? |

## Practical notes

- **AN**, **ET**, **EN**, **IR**, and **UR** work as subjects and objects; their spelling does not change.
- **NU** means “not” inside a clause. **NO** is the standalone answer “no.”
- **DA** marks past time; **VA** marks future time; **LO**, **PE**, and **SA** mark aspect.
- Use **LI** for “the,” **NA** for “a/an,” and **MA** for an unspecified plural.
- **PLE** comes before a request or command.
- The examples are standard grammatical phrases, not a claim that every one has become a fixed cultural idiom.
