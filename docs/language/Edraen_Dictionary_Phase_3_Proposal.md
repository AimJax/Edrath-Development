# EDRAEN DICTIONARY — PHASE 3 PROPOSAL

**Status:** Historical proposal; approved in full  
**Scope:** Proposed additional core vocabulary  
**Proposed roots in this batch:** 66  
**Approved inventory before this batch:** 139 roots  
**Approval status:** Approved by creator; see [Phase 3 Approved Record](Edraen_Dictionary_Phase_3.md)

## Purpose and canon boundary

This file is retained as the historical review draft. The creator approved all 66 roots; the canonical record is [Edraen Dictionary — Phase 3](Edraen_Dictionary_Phase_3.md). The approved master inventory and Canon Registry supersede this draft.

The proposals aim to extend the language beyond nouns into everyday actions, qualities, relationships, and common materials. Definitions are lexical suggestions only; they do not establish lore, cultural practices, or facts about Edrath.

## Semantic and spelling review notes

- These proposals were checked for exact-spelling collisions against the 139-root master inventory and the established whole terms and proper names listed in the current project canon. No exact-spelling collisions were found.
- Related opposites are intentional rather than duplicates: **DUM** (small) / **THRAN** (large); **RAL** (fast) / **METH** (slow); **KETHR** (hot) / **VELK** (cold); **TARV** (high) / **KOL** (low); **NARV** (near) / **URV** (far); **VELN** (full) / **NEMR** (empty); **GREL** (clean) / **ZERK** (dirty); **SETH** (good) / **GAVN** (bad).
- **MERV** means make/create generally; **DURN** means build/construct specifically.
- **MUR** means sleep; **MURK** remains the approved root for dream.
- **KAVTH** means companion; **KARN** remains the approved broader kin/family concept.
- Similar-looking roots are not exact duplicates, but any pair that feels too close in sound should be revised or removed during review.
- Pronunciation guides are practical reader aids. The full consonant inventory, permitted syllable shapes, and consonant-cluster rules remain open language-design topics.
