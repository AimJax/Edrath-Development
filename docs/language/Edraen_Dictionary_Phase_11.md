Phase 11 is a targeted vocabulary addition prompted by a translation stress test. The existing inventory could express sorrow and care, but it lacked a direct verb for pitying someone. This root fills that lexical gap; its transitive frame and the sentence's direct-address structure are documented in the lexical-frame supplement and Standard Grammar v3.3.

| Root | Approved meaning | Pronunciation guide |
|---|---|---|
| **RAETH** | Pity; feel compassionate concern for someone who is suffering or in hardship, with a wish that their distress be eased | rayth (AE as in “day”; TH as in “thin”) |
