## Purpose and canon boundary

This file records the 65 roots approved by the creator in the sixth Edraen vocabulary batch. All entries below are canon and are included in the master dictionary and Canon Registry.

This batch is designed to bring the core dictionary to the current milestone of 400 roots. It adds practical verbs, tools and household objects, natural materials and living things, physical qualities, and community roles. Definitions are lexical suggestions only; they do not establish lore, cultural practices, or facts about Edrath.

## Review notes

- The 65 spellings were checked against the previous 335-root inventory and established whole terms and proper names. No exact-spelling collisions were found.
- **Sound-similarity audit:** NIRV/NURV, NORK/NURK, KURM/KURV, VETHR/DETHR/KETHR, KULK/KULD/KULN/KULR/KULTH, RULTH/RULK/RULN, GULN/GULR/GULTH, ZULV/ZUL/ZULN, BARN/BREN, GAVR/GAVN, TALN/TAL/TALV, and VIRL/VIRN are close in form but not exact duplicates. These similarities were noted when the batch was approved.
- ZULV means violet, a narrower blue-purple color; ZULN means purple more broadly. NORK (flask) and BULK (barrel) are both containers but differ in typical size and use.
- Pronunciation guides remain practical aids rather than a complete phonological specification. Syllable shapes and consonant-cluster rules remain open topics.
