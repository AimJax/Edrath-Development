## The Oath of the Unbroken King

### 1. Birth, kneeling, and standing

**English**

I was not born to kneel before the world. I was born to stand before it when every other soul has fallen.

**Edraen**

AN NU DA KAVM TO HULK VULR LI RATH. AN DA KAVM TO DALK VULR IR KE AL MA OTH SURN PE FALN.

**Literal structure**

I / not / past / be-born / to / kneel / before / the world. I / past / be-born / to / stand / before it / when / all / plural / other / souls / past / fall.

### 2. Crowns, banners, wisdom, and honor

**English**

Let kings keep their crowns. Let armies raise their banners. Let the wise speak their wisdom and the proud sing of their honor.

**Edraen**

LET MA KAVT KAVN UR MA TULV. LET MA THRUM LAVK UR MA RULM. LET LI MA ZOVRN DAN FARL UR ZOVR AND LI MA GRAKV DAN THOLK OF UR GRAK.

**Literal structure**

Let / plural sovereigns / keep / their plural crowns. Let / plural armies / raise / their plural banners. Let / the plural wise people / speak / their wisdom / and / the plural proud people / sing / of / their honor.

### 3. Need, theft, and will

**English**

I have no need to steal what belongs to others. My will is enough.

**Edraen**

AN NU DOVT TO TOVR HURTH OF MA OTH DAN. AN VARN ES NULTH.

**Literal structure**

I / not / need / to / steal / property / of / plural / other / people. My will / is / sufficient.

### 4. War, fear, and resolve

**English**

When war comes, I will not hide behind stone walls. When fear spreads, I will not surrender my name. When the last light fades and the shadows swallow the land, I will still be standing.

**Edraen**

KE LI THAK TERV, AN NU VA NURTH BEH MA KHAR KARTH. KE GRATH DOVM, AN NU VA MULR AN NAG. KE LASK LOR FALM AND MA SKAR MULV LI KEL, AN VA LO STIL DALK.

**Literal structure**

When / the war / comes, I / not / future / hide / behind / plural stone walls. When / fear / spreads, I / not / future / surrender / my name. When / last light / fades / and / plural shadows / swallow / the land, I / future / ongoing / still / stand.

### 5. Weapons, command, and belief

**English**

My enemies may carry weapons of iron. They may command thousands. They may believe that strength belongs to whoever can take the most lives. Let them believe it.

**Edraen**

AN MA THARN MO BRAV MA THORG KRAK. UR MO KURT MORL MIL MA DAN. LET UR BIRN THA KARD ES OF LI DAN TA TALR KORL SU MA ZAR.

**Literal structure**

My plural enemies / may / carry / plural iron weapons. They / may / command / many thousands / plural people. Let / them / believe / that / strength / belongs to / the person who / can / take / the most / plural lives.

### 6. The meaning of victory

**English**

For there is a difference between the one who wins a battle and the one who decides what the battle means.

**Edraen**

ES MULK BET LI DAN TA ZAVT NA THAKM AND LI DAN TA RULR THAUL OF LI THAKM.

**Literal structure**

Because / there is / a difference / between / the person who wins a battle / and / the person who decides / the meaning / of / the battle.

### 7. The end of an age

**English**

I do not fear the end of an age. I am the reason the next one begins.

**Edraen**

AN NU GRAV LI VAK OF NA MARN. AN ES LI ZALR THA NETHV MARN KADL.

**Literal structure**

I / not / fear / the end / of / an age. I / am / the reason / that / the next age / begins.

### 8. Memory, silence, and the final answer

**English**

Remember my words when the banners burn. Remember them when the mighty fall silent. And when the world asks who remained, do not answer with the names of kings.

**Edraen**

SAVN AN MA THALN KE MA RULM GOTH. SAVN UR KE LI MA THRAN DAN FALN SULR. AND KE LI RATH THAVN KI MURV, NU RAKH MI MA NAG OF MA KAVT.

**Literal structure**

Remember / my plural words / when / plural banners / burn. Remember / them / when / the plural great people / fall / quiet. And / when / the world / asks / that / who / remained, do not / answer / with / plural names / of / plural sovereigns.

### 9. The name

**English**

Answer with mine.

**Edraen**

RAKH MI AN NAG.

**Literal structure**

Answer / with / my name.

## Validation notes

- The translation uses **KAVM** (be born), **HULK** (kneel), **DALK** (stand), **KAVT** (sovereign), **TULV** (crown), **THRUM** (army), **RULM** (banner), **THAUL** (meaning), and other recorded roots rather than inventing ad hoc translations.
- **AL** means all/every; **OTH** means other; **BEH** means behind; **BET** means between; **LET** marks a permissive command; **STIL** means still/continuing.
- **DA** marks past, **VA** future, and **NU** negation. Roots retain their dictionary forms.
- **MA** marks plural noun phrases; **OF** expresses a relation such as possession; **TA** introduces a relative clause; **THA** introduces a content clause.
- The English phrase “Let them believe it” is rendered as a permissive clause. The oath is a translation test, not a claim that its wording is a traditional Edraen cultural artifact.
- The translation is grammatically structured under the documented standard. If later testing reveals an ambiguity, revise the rule in the grammar guide and then update the translation consistently.
