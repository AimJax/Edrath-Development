# EDRAEN DICTIONARY — MASTER INVENTORY

**Status:** Approved Canon  
**Purpose:** Canonical cumulative reference for every approved Edraen root  
**Inventory:** 139 approved roots  
**Root batches:** 7 foundational roots + 66 Phase 1 additions + 66 Phase 2 additions

## Canon boundary

This is the canonical master reference for exact root spellings, meanings, and reader-friendly pronunciation guides. Roots are listed alphabetically so exact spelling can be audited efficiently. Batch histories remain available in [Phase 1](Edraen_Dictionary_Phase_1.md) and [Phase 2](Edraen_Dictionary_Phase_2.md).

Root definitions are lexical only. They do not independently establish religions, metaphysics, histories, social institutions, cultural practices, or facts about the peoples of Edrath.

## Approved pronunciation conventions

- Core vowels: A, E, I, O, U.
- AE is approximately the vowel in English “day”; AU is approximately the vowel in English “now.”
- TH is pronounced as in English “thin.”
- KH is pronounced like the final consonant in Scottish “loch.”
- NH in NHUR is pronounced as N.
- Default stress falls on the penultimate syllable, except that established proper names retain their recorded pronunciation guides.
- The guides in the table are practical reader aids, not IPA or a complete phonological specification.
- Permitted syllable shapes and the full consonant-cluster rules have not yet been established.

## Lexical distinctions

- **DUR** = soil or earth as material; **KEL** = land or territory; **RATH** = world or encompassing realm.
- **KORV** = wood as material; **TOL** = tree.
- **KELT** = plant root; **ZAL** = seed; **MEL** = flower; **ZAV** = grass.
- **MAV** = animal as a broad category; **ZIVR** = fish; **GUV** = insect; **KESH** = reptile; **VOG** = bird.
- **VETH** = ice; **KHAL** = snow; **ZUR** = rain; **SUL** = fog or mist.
- **DAK** = day; **RUK** = time; **MARN** = age/span of time; **KAD** = beginning; **ED** = primordial/original.
- **KREN** = mind; **TAV** = thought; **GREM** = memory; **VARN** = will; **THRUK** = knowledge; **ZOVR** = wisdom.
- **DASK** = law; **KURV** = authority; **THREN** = group of people; **DAN** = individual person.
- **MALK** = metal generally; **THORG** = iron specifically.
- **KREG** = craft; **ZEG** = skill; **RAG** = work.
- **LETH** = love/deep affection; **YAR** = joy/happiness; **GRATH** = fear; **VURN** = anger; **METHR** = sorrow/grief.
- **SURN** is a word for spirit/soul; its existence or nature is not established by the vocabulary entry.
- **DAN** refers to an individual person. Whether it applies to any particular kind of being depends on the wider setting.
