# EDRAEN DICTIONARY — PHASE 10

**Status:** Approved Canon  
**Batch size:** 25 new roots

Phase 10 adds practical vocabulary for time of day, housing, work, health care, transport, computers, and digital communication. Every spelling was checked against the prior 603-root inventory, the grammar-word ledger, and the other Phase 10 additions. Similar sounds are allowed; exact spelling duplicates are not.
