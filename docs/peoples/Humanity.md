# EDRATH — HUMANITY

**Document:** Human Foundation and Subrace Design  
**Version:** 0.2  
**Status:** Approved Core Premise — Some Lore Deferred

## 1. HUMANITY'S PLACE IN EDRATH

Humanity is one of the foundational peoples of Edrath and is canonically the setting's **“best” race**. Human superiority is a fundamental truth of the world from the Age of the Beginning onward; it is not something humans earned later through conquest, achievement, or political success. This premise must not be silently replaced with a more egalitarian fantasy convention.

Humans hold the dominant position across much of Edrath's established political and institutional life. Most widely known great kings and queens, major noble houses, governments, and law-enforcement institutions are predominantly human-led. The degree and form of this dominance varies by continent; it does not mean every country or institution must be exclusively human.

Humanity's overall superior status is established as a fact of Edrath, but it does not mean humans must be smarter or physically stronger than every other people, or best at every individual ability. Other races may surpass humans in particular traits or specialties without negating humanity's overall superiority. The underlying lore, cause, and metaphysical or historical explanation for this truth are deliberately deferred for later development; do not invent an explanation prematurely.

## 2. HUMAN APPEARANCE

Humans remain recognizably human. They do not need compulsory magical markings, unusual eyes, or invented anatomy merely to make them feel like a fantasy people.

Humanity has substantial variation in appearance, including skin tone, facial features, hair, height, build, and other ordinary human traits. Regional populations may develop recognizable appearances, but a country or culture should not be reduced to one uniform look.

## 3. HUMAN SUBRACES

Humans have multiple subraces. Each subrace is intended to have its own combination of:
- Distinctive physical features and appearance.
- Specialties or characteristic strengths.
- Other defining traits that make it recognizable within humanity.

These differences should be designed deliberately. A subrace should not be defined by a single superficial cosmetic change alone, nor should every member be forced into the same personality, occupation, culture, or moral role.

### Dravkhar — The Preeminent Human Subrace

**Name status:** Chosen and approved.  
**Design status:** Core subrace design approved.  
**Edraen etymology:** Deferred until the full Edraen dictionary is developed. No literal root-by-root meaning is established yet.

Dravkhar is the preeminent human subrace: across Edrath's history, it is the lineage most associated with great and historically consequential people. It is the recurring lineage associated with figures remembered as great rulers, leaders, founders, and other major historical figures. The lore explaining this historical prominence is intentionally deferred.

#### Defining Potential

Dravkhar have unusually broad potential across virtually any field humans can pursue in Edrath. This may include physical disciplines, intellectual pursuits, craftsmanship, leadership, the arts, and other fields. If magic or another extraordinary discipline is accessible to humans under the world's rules, a Dravkhar individual may have the potential to excel in it; membership does not grant automatic magical ability or access to powers humans otherwise cannot possess.

Potential exists on a broad spectrum, from ordinary capacity through gifted and exceptional ability to rare, extraordinary outliers. Many Dravkhar live entirely ordinary lives. Membership does not guarantee talent, achievement, mastery, greatness, social status, or leadership.

The defining advantage is an expanded range of what an individual might ultimately achieve, not automatic success. Realized achievement depends on individual aptitude, effort, training, experience, circumstances, and opportunity. Exceptional individuals usually develop one primary field of excellence; being world-class in several unrelated fields is not the default.

The highest outliers can stretch expectations far beyond ordinary human standards, but their capabilities remain finite and depend on the individual, field, circumstances, and rules of Edrath. There is no universal numerical ceiling. Excellence in one field does not imply excellence in another, and Dravkhar are not limitless.

Potential manifests variably. It may become evident early, develop gradually, or emerge under pressure; no single trigger or path applies to everyone. Appearance, family reputation, personality, and early performance do not reliably reveal an individual's ultimate potential. Setbacks do not automatically erase the underlying capacity, and recovery is usually possible, but this does not mean every injury or limitation can be overcome.

The underlying quality responsible for this potential remains undefined. It has not been decided whether it is biological, magical, metaphysical, or something else. Do not invent an explanation before it is discussed.

#### Appearance and Visual Signature

The intended visual direction is broadly European-associated, with a recognizable but natural inherited appearance:
- Hair ranges from blond through light brown, with a broad range of natural shades.
- Blue, green, and hazel eyes are predominant, while other natural eye colors remain possible.
- Skin is mostly fair, with variation.
- Subtle recurring facial resemblances contribute to the group's recognizable appearance without making members look identical.
- The combination of hair, eyes, skin, and facial resemblance creates the visual signature; no single trait is a mandatory checklist.

The overall aesthetic tends toward classical attractiveness, but not every member is guaranteed to be conventionally attractive. Individual appearance varies, and there are only loose recurring patterns rather than rigid appearance categories. A Dravkhar individual may lack much or even most of the full visual signature and still belong to the subrace.

Individual traits can occur in other human subraces; the full combination is uncommon outside Dravkhar. Appearance does not predict potential or determine membership.

#### Ancestry and Inheritance

Dravkhar membership follows ancestry, not a mandatory appearance test. The defining potential is distributed throughout the subrace and is not exclusive to one prestigious family or bloodline. Particular bloodlines may have distinctions of their own, but those distinctions are a separate topic for later development.

The visual traits tend to persist through generations but vary in inheritance. Descendants may inherit the full signature, some of its features, or little visible resemblance. Mixed ancestry with other human subraces is possible; no fixed ancestry percentage or generational cutoff has been established. Membership does not require a specified number of generations or a minimum visible trait count.

Dravkhar otherwise have ordinary human biology: no default advantage in strength, speed, stamina, health, or lifespan; no additional inherent supernatural power; ordinary human aging; and ordinary reproductive compatibility with other human subraces. Their defining distinction is potential, not a separate baseline of physical or supernatural capability.

#### Population and Temperament

Dravkhar are a moderately common part of humanity, with their population more concentrated in several regions. No particular continent, country, or ancestral homeland has yet been assigned.

A few mild psychological tendencies are approved: determination and persistence when pursuing important goals, along with mild competitiveness when testing abilities or facing challenges. These are tendencies, not universal personality traits. They do not mean every Dravkhar is ambitious, seeks power, or wants to lead. Predisposition interacts with upbringing, culture, experience, and personal choice.

#### Canon Boundaries

The name **Dravkhar** is settled, but its literal Edraen etymology is not. The existing root **KHAR** means “stone”; it must retain that meaning unless the language is deliberately revised. The name's final word construction and literal translation will be revisited during full Edraen dictionary development.

The subrace's core design above is approved. Its supporting lore, origins, historical explanation, exact regional distribution, and any distinctions among particular bloodlines remain deferred. No explanation involving gods, villains, or major events is established by this design entry.

## 4. DESIGN FRAMEWORK FOR OTHER RACES

The human subrace model is also a general starting framework for the other peoples of Edrath. Other races may have subraces with distinct appearances, specialties, and defining traits, where that structure fits the race.

This is a flexible design approach, not a requirement that every race must have the same number of subraces or use the same categories of difference.

For all races, keep these concepts distinct:
- **Race:** a people with a shared underlying identity or nature.
- **Subrace:** a meaningful division within a race, distinguished by a coherent set of traits.
- **Culture and nationality:** ways of life and political belonging, which do not have to map one-to-one onto race or subrace.

A race or subrace can live outside its ancestral homeland, and no population should be treated as culturally or morally uniform.

## 5. OPEN QUESTIONS

The following points remain open:
1. The underlying explanation and supporting lore for humanity's already-established primordial superiority; this is deferred for later discussion.
2. The precise historical scope of humanity's role as a founding people.
3. The number, names, and identities of the other human subraces.
4. Dravkhar's formal Edraen etymology and literal compound analysis, to be revisited after the full Edraen dictionary is developed.
5. Dravkhar's supporting lore, origins, exact regional distribution, and the reasons for its historical prominence.
6. Whether and how particular Dravkhar bloodlines differ from one another.
7. Further details of how human subraces relate to one another and to the other peoples of Edrath.

Develop these decisions collaboratively and record them in the project repository. Do not fill the open questions with assumptions before they are agreed.
