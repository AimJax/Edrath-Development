# EDRATH — GEOGRAPHIC TERMS AND THE FIVE CONTINENTS

**Document:** Geographic Naming Foundation  
**Version:** 1.2  
**Status:** Approved Foundational Canon

## 1. PURPOSE

Edrath has five major continents, each with an approved proper name and an approved broad climate and geographic identity. Edraen provides general terms for geographical and political concepts.

The five continent names, broad continental profiles, and initial set of 20 country names are established. Detailed maps, exact feature locations, country borders, peoples, cultures, civilizations, histories, and political structures remain open.

**Core principle: Distinct names. Distinct environments. Consistent language.**

## 2. GENERAL GEOGRAPHIC TERMS

### DRAUMIR — COUNTRY

**Draumir** is the Edraen word for “country” or “political territory.”

It is a general geographical and political term, not the proper name of one specific country. Depending on context, it may refer to a country, homeland, nation, or realm. The word alone does not specify whether the territory is a monarchy, republic, empire, city-state, or another political structure.

**Draumir** is an approved whole vocabulary item. No root-by-root etymology is assigned to it.

### ULMORA — CONTINENT

**Ulmora** is the Edraen word for “continent.”

It is a general geographical term, not the proper name of one specific continent.

**Ulmora** is an approved whole vocabulary item. No root-by-root etymology is assigned to it.

## 3. THE FIVE CONTINENTS

Edrath has five major continents. All five proper names and their pronunciation guides are approved canon.

### 3.1 KALDURA
- **Pronunciation:** kal-DOOR-ah
- **Category:** Continent
- **Broad climate and geography:** Highland terrain, mountain chains, grasslands, and strong seasonal variation.
- **Status:** Approved Canon

### 3.2 VESKARA
- **Pronunciation:** ves-KAR-ah
- **Category:** Continent
- **Broad climate and geography:** Arid deserts and plateaus, seasonal rivers, and oases.
- **Status:** Approved Canon

### 3.3 OTHMERE
- **Pronunciation:** OTH-meer
- **Category:** Continent
- **Broad climate and geography:** Temperate climate with four distinct seasons, fertile plains, river systems, and old forests.
- **Status:** Approved Canon

### 3.4 SYLVARIS
- **Pronunciation:** sil-VAIR-iss
- **Category:** Continent
- **Broad climate and geography:** Humid lowlands, lush rainforests, and rivers.
- **Status:** Approved Canon

### 3.5 UMBRAVA
- **Pronunciation:** um-BRAH-vah
- **Category:** Continent
- **Broad climate and geography:** Cold rugged terrain, glacial regions, volcanic ranges, and severe coastal weather.
- **Status:** Approved Canon

The five names are treated as unanalyzed proper names. No root-by-root meanings or etymologies are assigned to them in current canon.

The broad environmental profiles are approved, but they do not establish exact continent shapes, relative positions, coastlines, or the placement of individual features. See [Continental Climate and Geography](Continental_Climate_and_Geography.md) for the full profiles and scope limits.

The current plan assigns four named countries to each continent, for **20 countries total**. The complete list and naming policy are recorded in [Countries of the Five Continents](Countries_of_the_Five_Continents.md).

| Continent | Number of named countries |
|---|---:|

| **Total** | **20** |

This is a deliberate scope choice intended to make each country substantial enough to develop in detail. The count can be revised later if the setting has a clear need for more countries.

## 5. NAMING PRINCIPLES

1. Each continent has an approved, distinct proper name.
2. Names should not rely excessively on repeated sounds or endings.
3. Approved pronunciation guides should remain consistent.
4. General geographical terms and proper names must remain distinct.
5. The five continent names are canon as whole names; no etymology should be invented for them.
6. Country names do not need to have a literal meaning in Edraen.
7. Some country names may have origins in Edraen, older languages, or historical naming traditions; no origin should be assumed unless deliberately established.
8. Edraen compound rules govern words intentionally built from Edraen roots, not every proper name in Edrath.
9. A name does not establish the geography, culture, history, or political structure of the place.

## 7. FUTURE GEOGRAPHICAL DEVELOPMENT

The following content remains unwritten by design:

- The continents' relative positions, distances, exact shapes, sizes, and coastlines.
- Detailed maps and the precise locations of mountain ranges, rivers, forests, glaciers, deserts, and other features.
- Detailed biome boundaries and regional climate variations beyond the approved broad profiles.
- Country borders, size, and precise placement within each continent.
- Governments, political histories, and relationships between countries.
- Peoples, races, cultures, languages, religions, and civilizations.
- Historical events and relationships between regions.

Do not assume any of these details from a place's name. The broad climate and geography profiles are established; develop the remaining details deliberately and record them in the repository.

## DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

The continents of Edrath should be recognizable as parts of one world without feeling interchangeable. Their names, broad environmental identities, and initial country list are established; their precise geography, histories, cultures, and civilizations will be developed separately.

The worldbuilding takes inspiration from the scale and historical depth of *The Elder Scrolls* while building an original world with its own internal logic.

**Five continents. Twenty countries. Five distinct environments. One world. A history of our own.**
