# EDRATH — GEOGRAPHIC TERMS AND THE FIVE CONTINENTS

**Document:** Geographic Naming Foundation  
**Version:** 0.2  
**Status:** Approved Canon

## 1. PURPOSE

Edrath has five major continents, each with its own approved proper name. Edraen provides general terms for geographical and political concepts.

The names establish part of the world's identity. The continents' geography, cultures, civilizations, histories, and political structures will be developed separately.

**Core principle: Distinct names. Consistent language. Meaningful origins.**

## 2. GENERAL GEOGRAPHIC TERMS

### DRAUMIR — COUNTRY

**Draumir** is the Edraen word for “country.”

It is a general geographical and political term, not the proper name of one specific country.

Its precise political scope—such as whether it can refer to a sovereign nation, territory, homeland, or realm—may be refined as Edraen and Edrath's political concepts develop. Its status as the general word for “country” is approved.

### ULMORA — CONTINENT

**Ulmora** is the Edraen word for “continent.”

It is a general geographical term, not the proper name of one specific continent. The word may acquire cultural or historical associations through usage, but no literal root-by-root etymology has been established.

## 4. GENERAL TERMS AND PROPER NAMES

The distinction is:

- **Ulmora** — a continent, as a general concept.
- **Kaldura** — the proper name of one continent.
- **Veskara** — the proper name of one continent.
- **Othmere** — the proper name of one continent.
- **Sylvaris** — the proper name of one continent.
- **Umbrava** — the proper name of one continent.
- **Draumir** — a country, as a general concept.

The five continental names are approved independently of their etymologies. Their literal meanings and linguistic origins have not yet been established.

## 5. NAMING PRINCIPLES

1. Each continent has an approved, distinct proper name.
2. Names should not rely excessively on repeated sounds or endings.
3. Pronunciations should remain consistent.
4. General geographical terms and proper names must remain distinct.
5. Etymologies may be developed later, provided they remain consistent with Edraen's rules.
6. Approval of a name does not establish the place's geography, cultures, history, or political structure.

## DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

The continents of Edrath should be recognizable as parts of one world without feeling interchangeable. Their names are established; their geography, history, cultures, and civilizations will be developed separately.

The worldbuilding takes inspiration from the scale and historical depth of *The Elder Scrolls* while building an original world with its own internal logic.

**Five continents. One world. A history of our own.**
