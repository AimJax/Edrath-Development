# EDRATH — CONTINENTAL CLIMATE AND GEOGRAPHY

**Document:** Approved Continental Climate Profiles  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Approved Canon — Broad Profiles

## 1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE

The five continents of Edrath each have a distinct broad climate and geographic identity. These profiles are approved canon and establish the continents' large-scale environmental character.

They are deliberately broad. They do not yet establish exact coastlines, continent placement, mountain locations, river courses, biome boundaries, country borders, or the detailed geography of individual regions. Those details will be developed later without contradicting these profiles.

The peoples and races associated with each continent have not yet been decided. Climate should inform future worldbuilding, but it must not be used to predetermine a people's biology, culture, or political character.

## 2. KALDURA — HIGHLANDS, MOUNTAINS, AND GRASSLANDS

**Core identity:** A highland continent marked by major mountain chains and broad grasslands.

- **Climate:** Strong seasonal variation.
- **Broad geography:** Highland regions, mountain chains, and extensive grasslands.
- **Environmental character:** Elevation and seasonal changes give Kaldura a varied landscape rather than one uniform biome.

The precise location of its ranges, the extent of its grasslands, and its local climate zones remain open.

## 3. VESKARA — ARID LANDS AND DESERT PLATEAUS

**Core identity:** An arid continent shaped by desert environments, plateaus, seasonal rivers, and oases.

- **Climate:** Predominantly arid.
- **Broad geography:** Deserts and dry plateaus, with seasonal river systems and oases providing important concentrations of water.
- **Environmental character:** Water availability varies by place and season, creating contrast between dry expanses and river- or oasis-fed areas.

The location and scale of individual deserts, plateaus, rivers, and oases remain open.

## 4. OTHMERE — TEMPERATE PLAINS, RIVERS, AND OLD FORESTS

**Core identity:** A temperate continent with fertile plains, river systems, and old forests.

- **Climate:** Temperate, with four distinct seasons.
- **Broad geography:** Fertile plains, connected river systems, and extensive old forests.
- **Environmental character:** Seasonal change and the combination of arable lowlands, waterways, and mature forests give Othmere a varied temperate landscape.

The precise distribution of forests, plains, rivers, and local climate zones remains open.

## 5. SYLVARIS — LUSH RAINFORESTS AND HUMID LOWLANDS

**Core identity:** A lush, humid continent dominated by rainforest environments and lowlands crossed by rivers.

- **Climate:** Predominantly humid.
- **Broad geography:** Lush rainforests, humid lowlands, and substantial river systems.
- **Environmental character:** Dense vegetation and abundant surface water are defining features of its broad environmental identity.

The exact rainforest extent, river courses, elevation changes, and any local climate variations remain open.

## 6. UMBRAVA — COLD RUGGED LANDS, GLACIERS, AND VOLCANIC RANGES

**Core identity:** A cold, rugged continent combining glacial regions, volcanic ranges, and severe coastal weather.

- **Climate:** Predominantly cold, with harsh conditions in many regions.
- **Broad geography:** Rugged terrain, glacial areas, volcanic mountain ranges, and exposed coasts affected by severe weather.
- **Environmental character:** Ice, volcanic landscapes, difficult terrain, and storm-prone coasts create a harsh but geographically varied continent.

The activity and history of its volcanic regions, the extent of its glaciers, and the location of its mountain ranges and coasts remain open.

## 7. CONTINENTAL PROFILES AT A GLANCE

| Continent | Approved broad climate and geography |
|---|---|
| **Kaldura** | Seasonal highlands, mountain chains, and grasslands |
| **Veskara** | Arid deserts and plateaus, seasonal rivers, and oases |
| **Othmere** | Temperate climate, four seasons, fertile plains, river systems, and old forests |
| **Sylvaris** | Humid lowlands, lush rainforests, and rivers |
| **Umbrava** | Cold rugged terrain, glaciers, volcanic ranges, and severe coastal weather |

## 8. WHAT REMAINS OPEN

The approval of these profiles does **not** establish:

- The relative positions of the five continents or their distance from one another.
- Exact coastlines, shapes, sizes, or elevations.
- The precise placement and scale of individual mountain chains, rivers, glaciers, deserts, forests, or other features.
- Detailed biome boundaries, local climates, or seasonal patterns beyond those stated above.
- The placement of the 20 named countries, their borders, or their internal geography.
- The races, peoples, cultures, languages, religions, governments, economies, or histories of any continent.
- Whether any continent contains additional unusual environments that fit its broad profile.

## DESIGN PRINCIPLE

Each continent should feel environmentally distinct while still belonging to one coherent planet. These profiles provide a foundation for future maps, peoples, civilizations, and histories; they do not replace the need to develop those elements deliberately.
