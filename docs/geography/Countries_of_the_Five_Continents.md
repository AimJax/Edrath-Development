# EDRATH — COUNTRIES OF THE FIVE CONTINENTS

**Document:** Country Naming and Distribution  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Approved Canon — Names and Counts Only

## 1. PURPOSE

Edrath has five major continents and, in the current foundational plan, four named countries on each continent: **20 countries in total**.

This is a deliberate scope choice. Edrath does not need to reproduce the large number of modern nation-states found on Earth. A smaller set of countries leaves room to develop each country's identity, history, politics, and relationships in meaningful detail.

This document establishes country names and their continental grouping only. It does not establish borders, relative size, government, culture, population, history, or physical geography.

## 7. NAMING POLICY

- These 20 country names are approved proper names.
- A country's name does **not** need to have a known, literal meaning in Edraen.
- Some names may eventually have Edraen origins; others may come from older languages, historical naming traditions, or sources that are no longer understood. Such origins must not be assumed without a deliberate decision.
- Do not invent root-by-root etymologies for these names merely to make them fit Edraen's compound rules.
- Edraen's compound rules continue to govern words intentionally constructed from approved Edraen roots. They do not require every place name in Edrath to be an Edraen compound.

## 8. WHAT IS NOT YET ESTABLISHED

The following details remain open for future worldbuilding:

- Country borders, size, and exact placement within each continent.
- Forms of government, rulers, and political institutions.
- Cultures, peoples, languages, religions, and customs.
- Economies, settlements, military forces, and trade.
- Founding dates, historical events, conflicts, alliances, and relationships.
- Whether any country has changed names, split, merged, or ceased to exist during the world's history.

The grouping above is the current country plan, not a complete political map or a claim that these countries have existed unchanged throughout all of history.

## DESIGN PRINCIPLE

**Twenty countries to develop deeply, not dozens to maintain superficially.**

The count can be revised later if the setting genuinely needs it, but new countries should be added for a clear worldbuilding purpose rather than to fill space.
