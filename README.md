# Edrath Development

A living worldbuilding project for **Edrath**, its native language **Edraen**, and the world's geography, civilizations, history, and cosmology.

The project takes inspiration from the scale, historical depth, and layered worldbuilding of *The Elder Scrolls*, while developing an original setting, terminology, cultures, and history.

## Canon at a glance

- **Edrath** — the planet's proper name; canonically interpreted as “The Primordial World.”
- **Edraen** — the approved name of the planet's native language.
- **Draumir** — the Edraen word for a country or political territory.
- **Ulmora** — the Edraen word for a continent.
- **Rathaen** — the Edraen general term for “the eternal world.”
- **Five continents** — Kaldura, Veskara, Othmere, Sylvaris, and Umbrava.
- **Twenty named countries** — four assigned to each continent in the current country plan.
- **Five distinct continental profiles** — approved broad climate and geography for each continent.
- **Edraen dictionary** — 628 approved lexical roots with unique spellings: seven foundational roots, 393 additions in Phases 1–6, 85 additions in Phase 7, 66 additions in Phase 8, 52 additions in Phase 9, and 25 additions in Phase 10.
- **Humanity** — approved as a foundational people and the world's “best” race as a fundamental truth from the Age of the Beginning onward, not a status earned later. Humans dominate much of the world's major political and institutional life, with variation by continent. This does not mean humans must be smarter, stronger, or best at every individual ability.
- **Dravkhar** — chosen name of the preeminent human subrace. Its core design is approved: broad potential across human pursuits, a recognizable but non-mandatory visual signature, and ordinary human baseline biology. The supporting lore and formal Edraen etymology are deferred.
- **Other races and peoples** — the design workshop prioritizes recognizable fantasy races, with original races to be developed alongside them. Detailed definitions remain proposals until approved.

## Canon policy

- **Approved Canon** is established unless deliberately revised by the creator.
- Approved names and words do not require a root-by-root etymology. Where this project explicitly treats a term as an unanalyzed name or lexical item, do not invent an etymology for it.
- Country names do not need literal meanings in Edraen; place-name origins should only be added when deliberately established.
- The Canon Registry is the central reference for decisions. Keep all other documents consistent with it.
- Future development of borders, governments, cultures, histories, and other lore is content that has intentionally not been authored or approved yet, except for the human political foundation and Dravkhar core design recorded in the Canon Registry and Humanity document.
- Treat the approved broad climate and geography profiles as canon. Exact maps, detailed regional geography, peoples, and other unwritten lore must be developed deliberately and recorded in the repository.
- Documents marked **Working Draft — Proposals Only; Not Canon** are brainstorming material, not established facts about Edrath. Explicitly approved sections remain canon as identified.

## Design philosophy

**Simple rules. Distinctive names. Deep meanings.**

Edrath should feel like a world with its own inherited history and internal logic. Geography, language, culture, politics, religion, and history should reinforce one another. Inspiration is a starting point, not a template to copy.
