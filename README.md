# Edrath Development

A living worldbuilding project for **Edrath**, its native language **Edraen**, and the world's geography, civilizations, history, and cosmology.

The project takes inspiration from the scale, historical depth, and layered worldbuilding of *The Elder Scrolls*, while developing an original setting, terminology, cultures, and history.

## Canon at a glance

- **Edrath** — the planet's proper name.
- **Edraen** — the approved name of the planet's native language.
- **Draumir** — the Edraen word for a country.
- **Ulmora** — the Edraen word for a continent.
- **Five continents** — Kaldura, Veskara, Othmere, Sylvaris, and Umbrava.
- **Rathaen** — a working term for “world”; its etymology and compound structure remain unresolved.

## Canon and status conventions

- **Approved Canon** means the decision or name has been explicitly approved.
- **Working Canon** means it is the current working framework but may still be revised.
- **Provisional** means a proposal or explanation has not been approved as definitive.
- Approval of a proper name does not automatically approve a proposed etymology for that name.

The Canon Registry is the first place to check when deciding whether a detail is established. If a document and the Registry conflict, resolve the conflict explicitly rather than silently assuming one version is correct.

## Design philosophy

**Simple rules. Distinctive names. Deep meanings.**

Edrath should feel like a world with its own inherited history and internal logic. Geography, language, culture, politics, religion, and history should reinforce one another. Inspiration is a starting point, not a template to copy.
